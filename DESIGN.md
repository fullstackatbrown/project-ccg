# CCG client-facing website

Audience: prospective clients first, with clear paths for students and alumni. Preserve Contentful as the source of club facts, client engagements, services, values, members, and recruitment.

Full-screen photographic hero with the large cream CCG logo centered, no additional home hero copy. Crimson #8d0428 and tan #fae7bc, Aileron headings, original system sans-serif body. No accessory serif fonts.

Home sequence: full-screen centered-logo hero → pinned Who We Are / Our Team / Where We End Up sequence → annual report → footer. Client page has one combined engagement showcase with all clients selectable, followed by services, working approach, process, report and contact. No duplicate project section or historical-client grid.

Signature interaction: native-scroll pinned stages advance through the home people journey and three featured engagements on the Clients page. No wheel interception. Clients can select a case directly. Smaller screens and reduced-motion preferences get the home scenes in normal reading order and a selectable single-panel client showcase.

Rounded surfaces have subtle shadows and no outlines. No cream box or logo behind Who We Are. Values are editorial text, not icon cards with decorative red lines. Services are five fully visible rounded cards in one desktop row; they stack for smaller screens.

Navigation is a floating white rounded bar above full-viewport photography. Contact Us keeps cream text in active and hover states. Facebook is removed. Services are absent from the homepage. Standalone project-invitation blocks are removed. The Clients page includes the original public client logo portfolio before its pinned showcase; white client marks receive a dark surface. Values pair team photography with rounded icon cards without decorative red rules. Process steps reveal individually with consistent heading heights.

Pinned homepage headings sit at the top of their scene rather than vertically centering all content. Team photography uses contain to preserve the full photo; alumni marks and cards are larger. Intro statistics have selective crimson and blue surfaces. Client showcase defaults to the first three original projects with full Contentful descriptions, and displays descriptions directly without expansion controls. All public logo-wall cards have white backgrounds. Original labels Our Services, Our Values and Our Process are retained. Services use cream cards on crimson; process uses cream/white on blue with aligned descriptions. Report artwork fills its thumbnail area with no padding or decorative tint overlay.
